# bill

The appropriation acts. A bill is held separate from its [versions](../bill-version/) because
the bill is the persisting legislative vehicle while each version is a distinct artifact
carrying its own figures — and in this domain the figures change substantially between them.

Three bills are seeded, covering two of the four instrument families in the confirmed scope:

- **HB 96** — the FY2026-27 main operating budget, the current one.
- **HB 33** — the FY2024-25 main operating budget, its immediate predecessor and the
  comparator for any claim that funding rose or fell.
- **HB 54** — the FY2026-27 transportation budget, seeded to establish that "the Ohio budget"
  is not one bill.

The capital and workers' compensation families are in scope but unseeded at genesis. They
appropriate on different cycles and would each need their own fiscal-period and fund context
to be more than a stub.

Note that operating budgets are numbered fresh each General Assembly and that the same bill
number recurs across assemblies for entirely unrelated legislation. Filenames therefore carry
the General Assembly, not just the bill number.
