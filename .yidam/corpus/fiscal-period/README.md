# fiscal-period

Ohio appropriates by biennium but reports and closes its books by year, so every amount in
this corpus has to be stated against one or the other. A figure without a period is not a
figure — "the Medicaid line item is forty billion dollars" is not a claim until it says
whether that is one year, two years, general revenue only, or all funds.

The state fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30 and is named for the calendar year it ends in,
so FY2026 begins July 1, 2025. Biennia pair an odd-numbered year with the even year
following it and are enacted by the General Assembly seated that January.

Both period types are instances of the same class rather than separate classes, because the
relationship between them is containment rather than kind difference, and because
appropriation acts routinely state one figure for the biennium and separate figures for each
year within it.
