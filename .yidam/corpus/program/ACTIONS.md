# Actions — program

## Queries

- All line items funding a program, and each one's share
- Total program cost across funds and across line items, in a period
- Which jurisdictions a program distributes to, and by what formula
- Whether a program draws on earmarked revenue, and therefore whether its funding is
  genuinely in competition with other programs

## Transitions

- **Formula change.** The distribution rule changes, altering what recipients receive without
  necessarily altering the appropriation. Frequently the substantive policy change, and
  frequently invisible in the dollar figures.
- **Phase-in step.** A formula program moves to its next scheduled share. Distinguish a
  phase-in step from a policy change: the first was already decided, the second is new.

## Calculators

- [`per-capita`](../../skills/) — normalizes program distribution across recipients.
- [`real-dollars`](../../skills/) — required before comparing program cost across periods.

## Cautions

- Program cost and line item total are different figures. Report which one is being quoted.
- A phase-in that is not completed on the original schedule is a funding decision, but it
  will not appear as a cut in any year-over-year comparison. This is the most common way
  formula programs are reduced without a reduction being visible.
