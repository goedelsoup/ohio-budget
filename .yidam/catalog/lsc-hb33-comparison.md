## What it says about the FY2024 figures

Provision `EDUCD38`, Traditional school district funding formula. The House's +$627.0 million to
line item 200550 was not a change to the formula's shape but to its **inputs**: it updated the
base cost calculation from FY2018 data to FY2022 data, and required the FY2024 statewide average
base cost per pupil where the executive had required the FY2022 average. The document reports
the statewide average base cost per pupil rising to $8,241, an increase of 12.1% over the
$7,352 used for FY2022 and FY2023.

So the same programme, run on more recent and higher costs. The corpus's
[FY2026 counterpart](./lsc-hb96-comparison.md) records the opposite manoeuvre two years later —
the House there *froze* the base cost at the FY2024 average and redirected the formula's output
into "temporary foundation funding".

## Its figures are distribution estimates, as before

`Fiscal effect:` sentences quote allocations to traditional school districts, not appropriation
authority, and the same rule applies: never differenced against spreadsheet figures. See
[appropriation-is-not-distribution](../decisions/appropriation-is-not-distribution.yml), which
this document extends with a third failure mode — the corpus's line item is one **fund's** share
of a programme that draws on three.
