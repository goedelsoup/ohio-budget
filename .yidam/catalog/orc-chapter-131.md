# ORC Chapter 131

The statutory basis for several of the funds this corpus models, including the budget
stabilization fund and the local government fund, and for the formulas governing what is
credited to them. [inference]

## Why a statute entry matters more here than it looks

Most of the corpus's `[inference]` tags sit on statutory claims — which chapter authorizes an
instrument, what percentage of tax receipts a distribution fund receives, what a fund may
lawfully be spent on. These are the cheapest claims in the repository to verify, because
statute is public, stable, and unambiguous in a way that budget documents are not.

## The historical-text problem

[open] Whether superseded versions of these sections are retrievable is unresolved, and it
matters more than it appears. The corpus's window opens at FY2010, and the single most
consequential local-finance event inside it is the reduction of the local government fund's
share in the FY2012-13 budget. Verifying that requires the section **as it stood then**, not
as it stands now. A current-text-only source cannot establish the historical series, and the
claim would remain `[inference]` no matter how carefully the present text is read.
