## The weakest source in this catalog, and labelled as such

Wikipedia, not a legislative record. It is committed rather than cited by link precisely because
it is mutable: the bytes here are what the claims rest on, and a later edit cannot silently
change what this corpus asserted.

It was chosen after looking for something better. The Ohio House and Senate publish current
leadership, not a historical series; LSC's member directories are per-General-Assembly PDFs
without a consolidated leadership list. A single mutable page that states all four was preferred
over nine primary pages that state one each, and the tradeoff is recorded rather than hidden.

widely reported, which is an argument for confidence and not a substitute for provenance.

## What it is used for, and what it cannot support

Occupancy in `held_by` on [Speaker](../corpus/actor/speaker-of-the-house.yml) and
[President of the Senate](../corpus/actor/president-of-the-senate.yml).

It does **not** support a claim that leadership explains the legislative behaviour the corpus
measures. Four biennia is four data points, and the two candidate explanations move together —
see [leadership-and-the-anomalies](../decisions/leadership-and-the-anomalies.yml).
