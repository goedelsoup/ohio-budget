# ohio-budget

This repository tracks the Ohio state budget as a connected system — revenue instruments,
statutory funds, appropriation line items, the legislative and executive actions that move
them, and the state agencies and local jurisdictions the money reaches. It covers the full
appropriation surface across fiscal years 2010 to the present.

The central question is where the gap lies between what Ohio appropriates and what Ohio
actually spends, and what the record shows about how that gap arises.

## Read this first

Every dollar figure in this corpus is currently `[open]`. The genesis corpus establishes
*structure* — what kinds of things exist, how they connect, and what would have to be true for
a claim to be checkable — and deliberately does not assert amounts that no committed source
backs. The catalog is empty, so no claim carries a `[verified]` tag.

That is a design choice, not an oversight. A corpus that states precise, unsourced dollar
figures at line item granularity is worse than one that states none: the figures are citable,
they look authoritative, and nothing downstream can detect that they were remembered rather
than read.
